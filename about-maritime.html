Our Story

CodeAlytix was founded with a clear mission: to digitize maritime operations and build specialized software for the global shipping industry. We recognized that the maritime sector — one of the world's most critical industries — was underserved by generic software solutions that didn't understand the unique challenges of vessel operations, seafarer workflows, and international maritime regulations.

Our team combines deep technical expertise with real-world maritime knowledge to create solutions that simplify vessel operations and empower seafarers. From merchant navy exam preparation to AI-powered marine assistants, every product we build is designed specifically for the maritime world.

Mission & Vision

Our Maritime Products

We've built a suite of specialized maritime software products that address real challenges in the shipping industry:

1

iFleet — Fleet Management Platform

A comprehensive fleet coordination platform for shipping companies to manage crew sign-on/sign-off, vessel reporting, voyage tracking, and ship-shore communication.

2

Chief Mate Task — Shipboard Calculations

Ship-specific calculation software for deck officers covering trim calculations, ballast planning, draft survey, grain stability, and cargo calculations.

3

MMD Exam — Maritime Training Platform

A comprehensive exam preparation app for merchant navy officers with question banks, mock tests, maritime regulations, and progress tracking.

4

C-GPT — Marine AI Assistant

An AI assistant trained on maritime knowledge to answer SOLAS and MARPOL queries, assist with cargo calculations, and support marine professionals 24/7.
