Open Hearts Support Group

A safe place for anyone who feels overwhelmed, alone, or needs someone to talk to.

Hope • Healing • Support

Who This Group Is For

We welcome anyone who is struggling with life challenges and needs a kind, listening space. This group is for people dealing with depression, anxiety, anger issues, grief, loneliness, illness, cancer, burnout, stress, or simply feeling like they need support.

  • People feeling depressed or emotionally exhausted.
  • Anyone dealing with anger, frustration, or emotional overwhelm.
  • People facing cancer, illness, or major life changes.
  • Anyone who wants someone to listen without judgment.

What We Offer

We create a respectful and compassionate environment where people can speak freely, feel heard, and connect with others who understand.

  • Gentle, non-judgmental conversation.
  • Support for emotional healing and personal encouragement.
  • Access to listening ears, shared experiences, and practical guidance.
  • A community where no one has to carry pain alone.

How We Support Each Other

Every session is built around respect, privacy, and compassion. You can speak as much or as little as you feel comfortable with. The goal is not to fix everything instantly, but to help people feel seen, supported, and less alone.

  • Confidential and respectful group discussions.
  • Listening without blame, pressure, or judgment.
  • Encouragement, reflection, and peer support.

Important Note

This is a peer support and listening group. It is not a substitute for emergency care or professional medical treatment. If someone is in immediate danger, or if a medical emergency is happening, please contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional right away.
