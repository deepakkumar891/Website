Complete Fleet Management Solution

Crew Management

Streamline crew sign-on/sign-off processes, track certifications, manage crew schedules, and coordinate seamless crew changes across your fleet.

  • Digital crew records
  • Certificate tracking
  • Sign-on/off coordination
  • Crew scheduling

Vessel Reporting

Real-time vessel reporting system for noon reports, arrival/departure notifications, and operational updates from ship to shore.

  • Noon reports
  • Arrival/departure reports
  • Incident reporting
  • Performance monitoring

Voyage Tracking

Monitor vessel positions, track voyage progress, optimize routes, and manage port calls with integrated AIS data.

  • Real-time vessel tracking
  • Route optimization
  • Port call management
  • ETA calculations

Compliance Management

Ensure regulatory compliance with automated tracking of SOLAS, MARPOL, ISM Code, and other maritime regulations.

  • Regulatory compliance
  • Audit management
  • Document control
  • Inspection tracking

Maintenance Logs

Digital maintenance management system for planned maintenance, spare parts inventory, and equipment history tracking.

  • Planned maintenance
  • Work order management
  • Spare parts tracking
  • Equipment history

Ship-Shore Communication

  • Secure messaging
